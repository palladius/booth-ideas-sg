# Product Requirements Document: git-slider

## 1. Introduction

**git-slider** is a pioneering web application designed to revolutionize how presentations are shared and consumed. It addresses the growing need for dynamic, web-friendly, and version-controlled presentation content. By enabling users to effortlessly convert traditional slide decks (PowerPoint, PDF, Google Slides) into interactive GitHub Flavored Markdown (GFM) and publish them as responsive GitHub Pages sites, git-slider aims to empower presenters, educators, and developers to reach wider audiences with engaging and easily manageable content. Our goal is to transform static presentations into living documents that are accessible anywhere, anytime, with the power of version control.

## 2. Problem Statement

Presenters, especially in technical and educational fields, currently face significant challenges when trying to share their work in an accessible, interactive, and maintainable format. The traditional methods for sharing presentations suffer from several key pain points:

*   **Static and Inaccessible Content**: Sharing presentations often defaults to static PDF or PowerPoint files. These formats are not inherently interactive, provide a poor user experience on mobile devices, and limit engagement. They are difficult to embed gracefully and lack the dynamic capabilities of web content.
*   **Lack of Version Control**: Unlike code or documentation, presentations shared as static files lack robust version control. Updates require re-uploading entire files, leading to confusion about the latest version and making collaborative iteration cumbersome.
*   **Complex Web Development for Interactivity**: Creating truly interactive, web-based presentations typically requires significant web development skills, which most presenters do not possess. This creates a barrier to transforming engaging presentations into equally engaging web experiences.
*   **Poor Discoverability and SEO**: Static files are less discoverable by search engines compared to native web content, limiting the reach of valuable information. Content within images or non-text-searchable PDFs is particularly challenging for indexing.
*   **Inefficient Workflow**: The process of converting, optimizing, and publishing presentations for the web is often manual, time-consuming, and prone to errors, hindering productivity for individuals and organizations.

git-slider seeks to eliminate these pain points by offering a streamlined, automated, and intelligent solution.

## 3. Target Audience

The primary users of git-slider are individuals and professionals who frequently create and share presentations and desire a more efficient, interactive, and version-controlled method of publishing their content. This includes, but is not limited to:

*   **Developers & Technical Speakers**: Motivated to share technical concepts, code examples, and project updates in a format that integrates seamlessly with their existing GitHub workflows. They value markdown for its simplicity and version control for collaborative content. They often give presentations at conferences, meetups, or internal team meetings.
*   **Educators & Trainers**: Seeking to provide students with mobile-friendly, interactive learning materials that can be easily updated and accessed. They want to make their lessons more engaging and accessible outside of traditional classroom settings.
*   **Technical Writers & Documentarians**: Aiming to integrate presentation content directly into their documentation ecosystems, ensuring consistency and ease of maintenance alongside their codebases.
*   **Open-Source Project Contributors**: Looking for a simple way to create and share project overviews, tutorials, or workshop materials that live alongside their open-source projects on GitHub.
*   **Marketers & Product Managers**: Who want to share product roadmaps, feature presentations, or marketing collateral in a dynamic, web-based format that is easy to update and distribute.

Their shared motivations include streamlining their content publishing workflow, improving content accessibility and engagement, leveraging GitHub for version control and hosting, and enhancing the professional presentation of their ideas.
