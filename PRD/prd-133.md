# PRD: Culinary Compass - Your European Dining Journey Companion 🌍🍝

## 1. Introduction ✨

Welcome to Culinary Compass! This Product Requirements Document outlines the vision, goals, and detailed specifications for a groundbreaking mobile application designed to transform how culinary tourists remember, relive, and share their dining experiences across Italy, Greece, and Switzerland. Our aim is to build not just a logging tool, but a delightful digital scrapbook that captures the essence of every meal, making travel memories more vivid and shareable than ever before. We envision an app that blends intuitive design with a captivating "WOW factor" frontend and intelligent AI, providing a cherished companion for every foodie's European adventure. 💖📱

## 2. Problem Statement 😩📝

For enthusiastic culinary travelers exploring the rich gastronomic landscapes of Italy, Greece, and Switzerland, a significant pain point exists: the struggle to effectively remember, organize, and revisit their unique restaurant experiences. Current methods are often fragmented, uninspired, and fail to capture the sensory delight of dining abroad. Travelers often rely on scattered notes, generic photo galleries, or simply their fading memory, leading to:

*   **Loss of Detail:** Forgetting specific dish names, ambiance notes, service highlights, or the exact location of that hidden gem. 🤷‍♀️
*   **Disorganized Memories:** Photos are mixed with other travel pictures, notes are in different apps, making it hard to compile a coherent culinary story. 📸📂
*   **Lack of Inspiration:** No engaging way to browse past experiences or share a curated list of recommendations with friends and fellow travelers. 🗺️➡️
*   **Missed Opportunities:** Difficulty in discovering new restaurants based on past preferences or trusted recommendations. 🤔

## 3. Target Audience 🎯

Our primary target audience consists of **culinary tourists** and **food enthusiasts**, typically aged 28-55, who are passionate about exploring new cultures through their cuisine. They are:

*   **Tech-Savvy Travelers:** Comfortable using mobile apps to enhance their travel experiences, from booking flights to navigating new cities. 📱✈️
*   **Social Sharers:** Active on social media platforms like Instagram, where they enjoy sharing their dining experiences and discovering new places through their network. 📸🌐
*   **Detail-Oriented:** Appreciate the finer details of a meal – the ingredients, the presentation, the story behind a dish – and want to capture these nuances. ✍️
*   **Planners & Spontaneous Explorers:** Some meticulously plan their culinary journeys, while others delight in spontaneous discoveries. The app must cater to both. 🗺️❤️

Their motivation is to create a rich, lasting record of their gastronomic adventures, not just for personal nostalgia but also as a way to share their passion with a like-minded community.

## 4. Goals/Objectives 🚀

### User Goals (SMART)

*   **Effortless Logging:** Enable users to log a complete dining experience (photos, notes, ratings, location) in under 90 seconds for 85% of entries within 3 months of launch. ⏱️
*   **Engaging Playback:** Achieve a 40% user engagement rate with the "Dining Journey" map feature (viewing past meals on an interactive map) within 6 months. 🗺️
*   **Seamless Sharing:** Facilitate the sharing of at least 10,000 dining experiences to social media or direct contacts within the first year. 📲

### Business Goals (SMART)

*   **User Acquisition:** Acquire 25,000 active users across Italy, Greece, and Switzerland within the first 12 months. 📈
*   **High-Quality Content:** Generate over 100,000 user-created dining logs, with an average of 2+ photos per log, within the first year. 📸
*   **Partnership Readiness:** Develop a platform that is attractive for future partnerships with restaurants, travel agencies, and food bloggers by year two. 🤝

## 5. Features & Requirements 📋

### 5.1. User Authentication & Profile

*   **Description:** Secure and personalized access to the app.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   Social login (Google, Apple).
    *   User profile with name, photo, and a short bio.
    *   "My Culinary Passport" section showing stats (e.g., countries visited, meals logged).

### 5.2. Restaurant Log Creation

*   **Description:** The core feature for capturing dining experiences.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   **AI-Powered Restaurant Search:** Search for restaurants by name and location (powered by Google Places API).
    *   **Photo Upload:** Multi-image upload for dishes, ambiance, and menus.
    *   **Dish & Drink Logger:** Simple fields to add names of dishes and drinks.
    *   **Rating System:** 5-star rating for food, ambiance, and service.
    *   **Notes Section:** Free-text area for detailed memories and comments.
    *   **Tagging:** Ability to add tags (e.g., "romantic", "family-friendly", "local gem").

### 5.3. Interactive Dining Journey Map

*   **Description:** A visually stunning map that plots all logged dining experiences.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   Interactive map (Mapbox/Google Maps) with custom pins for each restaurant.
    *   Clicking a pin reveals a summary of the dining experience with a link to the full log.
    *   Filter by country, city, and rating.

### 5.4. Discovery & Social Sharing

*   **Description:** Features to discover new places and share experiences.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   "My Top Rated" list automatically generated on the user's profile.
    *   "Share to Social" button for individual logs (creates a beautiful summary image).
    *   "Send to a Friend" feature to share a log via messaging apps.

## 6. Tech Stack (Proposed) 💻

*   **Frontend:** Astro with React/Svelte components for interactivity.
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS for a modern, responsive design.
*   **Backend:** Firebase/Supabase for authentication, database, and storage.
*   **APIs:** Google Places API, Mapbox/Google Maps API.

## 7. Design & UX/UI Principles 🎨

*   **Visually Driven:** Emphasize user-generated photos with a clean, elegant layout.
*   **Intuitive & Fast:** The logging process must be seamless and quick.
*   **Delightful Micro-interactions:** Use subtle animations and feedback to make the app enjoyable to use.
*   **WOW Factor:** The Dining Journey Map should be a standout feature that users are excited to show off.
