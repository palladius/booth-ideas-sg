# PRD: Culinary Compass - Your European Dining Journey Companion 🌍🍝

## 1. Introduction ✨

Welcome to Culinary Compass! This Product Requirements Document outlines the vision, goals, and detailed specifications for a groundbreaking mobile application designed to transform how culinary tourists remember, relive, and share their dining experiences across Italy, Greece, and Switzerland. Our aim is to build not just a logging tool, but a delightful digital scrapbook that captures the essence of every meal, making travel memories more vivid and shareable than ever before. We envision an app that blends intuitive design with a captivating "WOW factor" frontend and intelligent AI, providing a cherished companion for every foodie's European adventure. 💖📱

## 2. Problem Statement 😩📝

For enthusiastic culinary travelers exploring the rich gastronomic landscapes of Italy, Greece, and Switzerland, a significant pain point exists: the struggle to effectively remember, organize, and revisit their unique restaurant experiences. Current methods are often fragmented, uninspired, and fail to capture the sensory delight of dining abroad. Travelers often rely on scattered notes, generic photo galleries, or simply their fading memory, leading to:

*   **Loss of Detail:** Forgetting specific dish names, ambiance notes, service highlights, or the exact location of that hidden gem. 🤷‍♀️
