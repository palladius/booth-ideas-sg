# Product Requirements Document: SmartStay Access Manager

## 1. Introduction

The SmartStay Access Manager is an innovative mobile application designed to revolutionize how short-term rental property hosts manage guest access and communication. This project aims to automate the entire process of generating, distributing, and revoking rotating access keys for smart locks, seamlessly integrating with popular booking platforms like Airbnb and Booking.com. Furthermore, it will automate personalized guest messaging, ensuring guests receive timely and accurate information from check-in to check-out. The ultimate goal is to significantly reduce the operational burden on hosts, enhance property security, and elevate the overall guest experience, leading to improved satisfaction and potentially increased bookings.

## 2. Problem Statement

Short-term rental hosts, whether individual property owners or professional managers, frequently grapple with a myriad of challenges related to guest access and communication. The current landscape is often characterized by:

*   **Manual Key Management:** The manual coordination of physical key handovers is time-consuming, prone to delays, and inflexible, especially for late-night arrivals or early departures. For smart locks, manually generating and distributing unique access codes for each guest is repetitive and error-prone.
*   **Security Vulnerabilities:** Reusing static access codes or physical keys for multiple guests poses significant security risks. Forgotten or lost keys can lead to unauthorized access, compromising property safety and guest peace of mind. The lack of rotating, time-bound keys means hosts must remember to manually change codes, which is often overlooked.
*   **Operational Inefficiencies:** Hosts spend an inordinate amount of time on administrative tasks, including sending welcome messages, check-in instructions, Wi-Fi details, and local recommendations. This detracts from their ability to focus on other aspects of property management or guest satisfaction.
*   **Inconsistent Guest Experience:** Lack of a standardized, automated communication process can lead to inconsistent guest experiences. Guests may receive information too late, find it difficult to access the property, or miss crucial details, leading to frustration and negative reviews.
*   **Integration Gaps:** Existing smart lock solutions often lack seamless integration with booking platforms, requiring hosts to manually transfer guest details and access times between systems.

These pain points collectively contribute to increased operational costs, host stress, and a potentially diminished guest experience, impacting the profitability and reputation of short-term rental businesses.

**Demographics & Characteristics:**
*   **Property Portfolio:** Ranging from individuals managing a single property to businesses overseeing multiple units.
*   **Tech Savvy:** Generally comfortable with mobile applications and smart home technology, but appreciate intuitive, user-friendly interfaces.
*   **Time-Conscious:** Highly value solutions that save them time and automate repetitive tasks.
*   **Security-Focused:** Concerned about property security and guest safety, looking for reliable access control solutions.
*   **Customer-Centric:** Aim to provide an excellent guest experience to secure positive reviews and repeat bookings.

**Motivations & Goals:**
*   **Reduce Administrative Burden:** Their primary motivation is to minimize the time and effort spent on key management and guest communication.
*   **Enhance Property Security:** They seek robust solutions that provide secure, temporary access codes for each guest, preventing unauthorized re-entry.
*   **Improve Guest Satisfaction:** They want to offer a seamless and professional check-in experience, providing guests with all necessary information proactively.
*   **Increase Operational Efficiency:** Streamline workflows, allowing them to scale their operations or dedicate more time to value-add activities.

*   **Competitive Advantage:** Offer a superior guest experience that differentiates their listings in a competitive market.

## 4. Goals/Objectives

Our SMART goals for the SmartStay Access Manager are:

*   **G1: Automate Key Distribution:** Achieve 95% automation of access key generation and distribution for new bookings within 1 month of full launch.
    *   **Measure:** Percentage of bookings where keys are automatically generated and sent without host intervention.
*   **G2: Reduce Host Manual Effort:** Decrease the average host's weekly manual time spent on key management and guest communication by 70% within 3 months of adoption.
    *   **Measure:** Survey host users on time savings and track system interaction data.
*   **G3: Enhance Guest Satisfaction:** Increase the average guest check-in experience rating by 0.5 points on a 5-point scale within 6 months of active use (as reported via post-stay surveys or booking platform reviews related to check-in).
    *   **Measure:** Guest feedback related to ease of access and clarity of instructions.
*   **G4: Improve Property Security:** Ensure all access keys are time-bound and automatically revoked upon guest checkout for 100% of bookings managed by the app within 1 month post-launch.
    *   **Measure:** Audit logs of key activation and deactivation timestamps.
*   **G5: Achieve Scalable Integration:** Successfully integrate with at least 3 major smart lock brands and 2 primary booking platforms (e.g., Airbnb, Booking.com) within the first 6 months post-MVP launch.
    *   **Measure:** Number of successful API integrations and active users utilizing them.

## 5. Features & Requirements

### 5.1. Automated Rotating Key Management

**Description:** This core feature will automatically generate unique, time-sensitive access codes for each guest, distribute them, and revoke them upon checkout. It will integrate directly with various smart lock systems.

**Why it's important:** Eliminates manual key handling, enhances security by ensuring codes are valid only for the booking duration, and reduces host operational burden.

**Functional Requirements:**
*   **FR1.1: Smart Lock Integration:** The system SHALL integrate with leading smart lock APIs (e.g., August, Yale, Schlage, etc.) to manage access codes.
*   **FR1.2: Booking Platform Sync:** The system SHALL automatically pull booking details (guest name, check-in/out dates, property ID) from connected Airbnb/Booking.com accounts.
*   **FR1.3: Unique Code Generation:** The system SHALL generate a unique, random access code for each guest reservation.
*   **FR1.4: Time-Bound Access:** The system SHALL set the access code to be active only from a specified check-in time to a specified check-out time (e.g., 3 PM on check-in day to 11 AM on check-out day).
*   **FR1.5: Automated Distribution:** The system SHALL automatically deliver the access code to the guest via automated messaging (email, SMS, or within booking platform message).
*   **FR1.6: Automated Revocation:** The system SHALL automatically deactivate or delete the access code from the smart lock at the designated check-out time.
*   **FR1.7: Manual Override:** Hosts SHALL be able to manually generate, modify, or revoke access codes if needed.
*   **FR1.8: Multi-Property Support:** Hosts SHALL be able to manage multiple properties, each with its own set of smart locks and booking configurations.

### 5.2. Automated Personalized Guest Messaging

**Description:** This feature will send pre-scheduled, personalized messages to guests, providing essential information at key points of their journey (pre-arrival, check-in, during stay, check-out).

**Why it's important:** Improves guest experience by providing timely information, reduces host communication workload, and minimizes guest inquiries.

**Functional Requirements:**
*   **FR2.1: Message Templates:** The system SHALL allow hosts to create and customize message templates for various stages (e.g., 'Booking Confirmation', 'Pre-Arrival Details', 'Check-in Instructions', 'Wi-Fi Info', 'Check-out Reminder').
*   **FR2.2: Dynamic Variables:** Message templates SHALL support dynamic variables (e.g., `{{guest_name}}`, `{{check_in_date}}`, `{{access_code}}`, `{{wifi_password}}`, `{{property_address}}`).
*   **FR2.3: Scheduled Delivery:** The system SHALL enable scheduling messages to be sent at specific times relative to check-in/out (e.g., '24 hours before check-in', '30 mins after check-in', '1 day before check-out').
*   **FR2.4: Multi-Channel Messaging:** The system SHALL support sending messages via email, SMS, and potentially direct integration with booking platform messaging APIs (if available and feasible).
*   **FR2.5: Message History:** Hosts SHALL be able to view a history of all messages sent to each guest.
*   **FR2.6: Emergency Messaging:** Hosts SHALL be able to send ad-hoc, urgent messages to current guests.
*   **FR2.7: Welcome Guide Integration:** The system SHALL allow hosts to embed or link to a digital welcome guide within messages, containing property specifics and local recommendations.

### 5.3. Host Dashboard & Management UI

**Description:** A intuitive mobile and web interface for hosts to oversee all properties, bookings, guest communications, and system settings. The UI will be a simple mock but colorful, focusing on clarity and ease of use.

**Why it's important:** Provides hosts with a central control panel for their operations, making the system accessible and manageable.

**Functional Requirements:**
*   **FR3.1: Property Overview:** The dashboard SHALL display a summary of all managed properties, upcoming bookings, and current guest status.
*   **FR3.2: Booking Management:** Hosts SHALL be able to view detailed booking information, including guest details, stay dates, and access code status.
*   **FR3.3: Smart Lock Status:** The dashboard SHALL display the current status of connected smart locks (e.g., battery level, last access event).
*   **FR3.4: Settings & Configuration:** Hosts SHALL be able to configure smart lock integrations, message templates, scheduling rules, and personal preferences.
*   **FR3.5: User Interface Design:** The application SHALL feature a clean, intuitive, and colorful user interface with clear navigation and visual cues to simplify complex tasks. (e.g., prominent 'upcoming bookings' card, color-coded status indicators for bookings/locks, use of pastel colors for sections and accent colors for actionable elements).
*   **FR3.6: Activity Log:** The system SHALL provide an activity log showing key events like access code generation, message delivery, and guest entry/exit (if supported by smart lock).

## 6. User Stories

Here are a few key user stories from the perspective of a short-term rental host:

*   **As a host, I want the app to automatically generate a unique, time-bound access code for each new booking and send it to the guest** so that I don't have to manually manage keys and can ensure property security.
*   **As a host, I want to create customizable message templates with dynamic guest information** so that I can provide personalized, consistent communication without typing out the same details repeatedly.
*   **As a host, I want to schedule welcome messages, check-in instructions, and Wi-Fi details to be sent automatically at specific times before and during the guest's stay** so that guests receive all necessary information proactively and I can focus on other tasks.
*   **As a host, I want the access code to automatically expire and be removed from the smart lock after the guest checks out** so that I don't have to worry about unauthorized re-entry and property security is maintained.
*   **As a host, I want to see a clear overview of all my upcoming bookings and the status of their access codes and messages** so that I can quickly verify everything is in order and intervene if necessary.

### 7.2. Proposed Tech Stack

*   **Frontend (Mobile App):** React Native for cross-platform compatibility, providing a fluid and colorful user experience. Alternatively, native iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Kotlin/Jetpack Compose) for optimal performance and native feel, especially for complex UI animations and specific device integrations.
*   **Frontend (Web UI):** React.js/Next.js for the host dashboard, ensuring a responsive and feature-rich interface.
*   **Backend:** Node.js (with Express.js or NestJS) or Python (with Django/Flask) for building scalable and efficient microservices. Emphasis on asynchronous processing for API calls.
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL for relational data (user accounts, property details, booking information, access codes) and potentially a NoSQL database (e.g., MongoDB, DynamoDB) for flexible storage of message templates or activity logs.
*   **Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS (EC2, Lambda, S3, RDS, SNS, SQS) or Google Cloud Platform (Compute Engine, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL, Pub/Sub) for scalability, reliability, and security.
*   **Authentication & Authorization:** OAuth 2.0 and JWT for secure API access.
*   **Smart Lock Integration:** Utilize official smart lock vendor APIs. Implement robust error handling and retry mechanisms due to potential external API unreliability.
*   **Messaging:** Integration with Twilio for SMS and SendGrid/Mailgun for email, or direct API integration with booking platforms where allowed.
*   **AI/Machine Learning:** Python (with libraries like TensorFlow/PyTorch, scikit-learn) for developing the AI models. This module will focus on:
    *   **Predictive Scheduling:** Optimizing key generation/distribution timings based on historical guest behavior and check-in patterns to preemptively handle potential issues.
    *   **Anomaly Detection:** Monitoring access patterns to identify unusual activity (e.g., multiple failed entry attempts, entry outside scheduled times) and alert hosts.
    *   **Personalized Communication Enhancement:** AI to refine message timing and content based on guest profiles and past interactions.
*   **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions or GitLab CI/CD for automated testing, building, and deployment.

### 7.3. Security Considerations

*   **Data Encryption:** All sensitive data (access codes, guest information) at rest and in transit will be encrypted.
*   **API Security:** Implement robust API authentication, authorization, rate limiting, and input validation.
*   **Least Privilege:** Access to smart lock controls and sensitive data will follow the principle of least privilege.
*   **Audit Trails:** Comprehensive logging of all critical system actions and access events.
*   **Vulnerability Scanning:** Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.

## 8. Potential Risks & Mitigations

### 8.1. Technical Risks

*   **R1: Smart Lock API Inconsistencies/Reliability:** Different smart lock vendors have varying API capabilities, reliability, and documentation quality.
    *   **Mitigation:** Prioritize integration with major, well-documented smart lock brands first. Implement a robust abstraction layer for smart lock integrations to minimize impact from individual vendor changes. Design with retry mechanisms, circuit breakers, and comprehensive error handling for API calls. Conduct thorough testing with each integrated lock.
*   **R2: Booking Platform API Restrictions/Changes:** Booking platforms may change their APIs or impose restrictions on automated messaging/data access.
    *   **Mitigation:** Monitor platform API documentation closely. Prioritize official partnership programs if available. Design modular integration adapters to easily update with API changes. For messaging, provide fallback options like direct email/SMS if platform messaging is restricted.
*   **R3: Scalability Challenges:** As user base and property count grow, the system might face performance bottlenecks.
    *   **Mitigation:** Employ a microservices architecture. Utilize cloud-native, auto-scaling services. Implement load testing and performance monitoring from early stages. Optimize database queries and caching strategies.
*   **R4: Security Vulnerabilities:** Handling access codes and personal guest data presents a high security risk.
    *   **Mitigation:** Adhere to industry best practices for data encryption, secure coding, and access control. Implement regular security audits, penetration testing, and maintain strong authentication protocols. Isolate sensitive data and use tokenization where possible.

### 8.2. Market & Business Risks

*   **R5: Competition from Existing Solutions:** Established property management systems or smart lock native apps might offer similar features.
    *   **Mitigation:** Differentiate through superior UX/UI (simple, colorful mock), deeper AI-powered automation (predictive scheduling, anomaly detection), and broader smart lock/booking platform integrations. Focus on solving the specific pain points of key rotation and messaging more effectively.
*   **R6: User Adoption:** Hosts might be reluctant to switch from existing manual processes or other tools.
    *   **Mitigation:** Focus on a seamless onboarding process and intuitive UI. Offer clear demonstrations of time and cost savings. Provide excellent customer support. Develop compelling marketing materials highlighting ease of use and benefits.
*   **R7: Monetization Strategy:** Finding the right pricing model to attract users while ensuring profitability.
    *   **Mitigation:** Start with a freemium model or a tiered subscription based on the number of properties/bookings. Conduct market research on competitive pricing. Gather user feedback on perceived value.

## 9. Success Metrics

Measuring the success of the SmartStay Access Manager will be multi-faceted, encompassing user engagement, operational efficiency, and overall business impact.

### 9.1. User Value Metrics

*   **Key Automation Rate:** Percentage of new bookings for which access codes are automatically generated and distributed without manual intervention (Target: >95%).
*   **Message Automation Rate:** Percentage of scheduled messages successfully sent automatically (Target: >98%).
*   **Host Time Savings:** Average reduction in time hosts spend on manual key management and guest communication per week (Measured via surveys and in-app activity tracking; Target: >70% reduction).
*   **Guest Check-in Feedback Score:** Average rating from guest surveys or booking platform reviews related to the ease and clarity of the check-in process (Target: Increase by 0.5 points).
*   **Feature Adoption Rate:** Percentage of active hosts utilizing key features like automated messaging and smart lock integration (Target: >80% for core features).
*   **Daily/Weekly Active Users (DAU/WAU):** Number of unique hosts actively using the application (Indicates engagement).

### 9.2. Business Value Metrics

*   **Churn Rate:** Percentage of hosts discontinuing their subscription or usage (Target: <5% monthly).
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Cost to acquire a new paying host.
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV):** Revenue generated from a single host over their entire relationship with the product.
*   **Number of Integrated Properties/Smart Locks:** Total count of properties and smart locks actively managed through the platform (Indicates market penetration and scalability; Target: >1000 properties within 1 year).
*   **Reduction in Support Tickets:** Decrease in support requests related to key access or communication issues (Target: >30% reduction post-implementation).
*   **Revenue Growth:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).
*   **Booking Platform Review Impact:** Track correlation between app usage and improvement in overall guest review scores on booking platforms.
