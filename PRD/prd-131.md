# Dungeons & Dragons Character Sheet Generator PRD

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the Product Requirements for a Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) Character Sheet Generator. The primary goal of this application is to streamline and enhance the initial character creation process for D&D players by automating ability score generation, providing compelling visual feedback, and allowing for personal character imaging. This tool aims to make character creation faster, more engaging, and visually intuitive, thereby lowering the barrier to entry for new players and offering a convenient utility for experienced players and Dungeon Masters alike.

## 2. Problem Statement

The traditional Dungeons & Dragons character creation process, particularly the generation of ability scores, can be a significant bottleneck and source of friction for players. Manually rolling three dice for each of the six main abilities (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) is repetitive, time-consuming, and prone to manual error. This process often lacks visual engagement, leading to a sterile experience where players simply record numbers without immediate visual context or excitement. Furthermore, integrating a personal character image into a basic sheet often requires external tools and manual effort, breaking the flow of character conceptualization. These pain points collectively detract from the initial fun and immersion of starting a new D&D campaign, making the foundational step of character creation feel more like a chore than an exciting beginning.

## 3. Target Audience

The primary target users for this product are Dungeons & Dragons players of all experience levels, from complete beginners to seasoned veterans and Dungeon Masters (DMs).

*   **New Players**: Individuals new to D&D who may be intimidated by the rules and manual processes of character creation. Their motivation is to quickly and easily get a character ready to play, reducing the learning curve and making the initial experience more welcoming and fun.
*   **Experienced Players**: Players who frequently create new characters for one-shot adventures, new campaigns, or to experiment with different builds. They seek efficiency and a visually satisfying experience to quickly iterate on character concepts without the tedium of manual rolling and recording.
*   **Dungeon Masters (DMs)**: DMs who need to rapidly generate statistics for Non-Player Characters (NPCs) or populate a campaign with quick character archetypes. Their motivation is speed and convenience, allowing them to focus on storytelling and campaign management rather than repetitive administrative tasks.

All target users share a common goal: to efficiently generate D&D character ability scores in a visually appealing and engaging manner, allowing them to personalize their characters with ease and get to the heart of gameplay faster.

## 4. Goals/Objectives

### Business Goals (SMART)

*   **User Acquisition**: Attract 10,000 unique monthly active users (MAU) within 6 months post-launch, as measured by application usage metrics.
*   **User Retention**: Achieve a monthly user retention rate of 30% after the first month, indicating sustained value for the user base.
*   **Feedback & Iteration**: Collect sufficient user feedback (e.g., through surveys or in-app prompts) from 10% of active users within the first 3 months to inform future product development and identify potential monetization avenues.
*   **Brand Recognition**: Establish the application as a go-to tool for D&D character creation, increasing brand visibility within the D&D community and laying a foundation for future product expansions.

### User Goals (SMART)

*   **Efficiency**: Reduce the average time spent on generating a full set of six ability scores by 75% compared to manual dice rolling, as measured by user task completion times in testing.
*   **Engagement**: Provide a visually immersive and satisfying dice-rolling experience that is rated 'Excellent' by at least 80% of surveyed users regarding visual feedback and interactivity.
*   **Personalization**: Enable users to successfully upload and display a character image with 95% success rate and provide positive feedback regarding the ease of customization.
*   **Guidance**: Offer intelligent suggestions for suitable D&D classes based on generated ability scores, with at least 60% of users finding these suggestions helpful in their character concept development.
