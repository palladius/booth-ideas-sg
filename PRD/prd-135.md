# Chrome Crash Counter Extension PRD

## 1. Introduction

The Chrome Crash Counter is a proposed browser extension designed to address the user frustration stemming from unquantified browser instability. This project aims to provide Chrome users with a simple, automated, and visually engaging way to track the frequency of their browser crashes. By offering a clear, real-time metric, the extension intends to transform anecdotal frustration into objective data, thereby helping users understand their browser's stability over time.

## 2. Problem Statement

Chrome browser crashes are an unwelcome and common occurrence for many users, disrupting workflows, leading to data loss, and causing significant frustration. Currently, there is no readily available, automatic, and user-friendly mechanism for individuals to quantitatively track how often their Chrome browser experiences a critical failure. Users often rely on subjective memory or manual tallies, which are unreliable and fail to provide an objective measure of the problem's magnitude. This lack of objective data prevents users from recognizing patterns, understanding the severity of their browser's instability, or effectively communicating the issue if they seek support. The pain points include:
*   **Unquantified Frustration:** Users know their browser crashes, but they don't know *how often*, leading to a vague sense of annoyance rather than actionable insight.
*   **Workflow Disruption:** Each crash interrupts productivity, forcing users to restart their browser and potentially lose unsaved work.
*   **Lack of Data for Troubleshooting:** Without a crash count, it's harder for users to identify if the issue is sporadic or persistent, or to provide concrete data when reporting problems.
*   **Emotional Burden:** Persistent, untracked crashes can lead to a feeling of powerlessness and heightened stress.

## 3. Target Audience

The primary target audience for the Chrome Crash Counter extension is any regular Chrome browser user who experiences sporadic or frequent crashes and desires an objective, easily accessible metric of their browser's stability. This includes:
*   **Productivity-Conscious Users:** Individuals who rely heavily on Chrome for work or personal tasks and are deeply affected by unexpected interruptions.
*   **Data-Curious Individuals:** Users who appreciate metrics and data to understand their digital environment, even for seemingly minor issues.
*   **Frustrated Users Seeking Validation:** Those who feel their browser crashes too often and want quantitative proof to support their experience.
*   **Non-Technical Users:** The solution must be intuitive and require no technical expertise to install or use, appealing to a broad user base beyond developers.

Their motivations and goals include:
*   **Understanding Stability:** Gaining a clear picture of their Chrome browser's reliability over time.
*   **Quantifying Frustration:** Turning a subjective feeling of annoyance into an objective, measurable statistic.
*   **Gaining Control:** Feeling more informed and in control of their browsing experience.
*   **Simplicity:** They seek a tool that is easy to install, passive in its operation, and clear in its display.

## 4. Goals/Objectives

Our primary goals for the Chrome Crash Counter are focused on user utility and experience:

*   **User Goal 1 (Visibility):** Provide users with an instant, clear, and engaging visual metric of their Chrome browser's stability over time, aiming for 95% user satisfaction with the clarity of the display within 3 months of launch (measured via in-app surveys or store reviews).
*   **User Goal 2 (Accuracy):** Accurately detect and count 99% of all genuine Chrome browser crashes for active users within the first month of installation.
*   **Business Goal 1 (Engagement):** Achieve an average daily active user (DAU) rate of at least 70% of total installations within the first six months, indicating sustained user engagement with the crash count display.
*   **Business Goal 2 (Platform Stability):** Ensure the extension itself operates with minimal resource consumption (less than 0.5% CPU idle, less than 30MB RAM) and zero self-induced crashes across 99.9% of user sessions.

## 5. Features & Requirements

### 5.1 Core Functionality: Automatic Crash Detection & Counting
*   **Description:** The extension will run silently in the background, constantly monitoring the Chrome browser's state. Upon detecting an unexpected browser termination or crash event, it will automatically increment a persistent crash counter.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   Must reliably detect browser-level crashes (not just tab crashes or extension failures).
    *   Counter must persist across browser restarts and system reboots.
    *   Detection mechanism must be lightweight and not impact browser performance.
    *   Utilize Chrome Extension APIs for crash detection where available and appropriate.
*   **Importance:** This is the foundational feature, directly addressing the problem of unquantified browser instability.

### 5.2 User Interface: Crash Count Display
*   **Description:** The current crash count will be prominently displayed to the user, typically via the extension's browser action icon (badge text) or a simple popup UI.
*   **Requirements:**
    *   Display the current total crash count clearly.
    *   Optionally, display a small, relevant emoji alongside the count (e.g., a 😢 for a high count, a 😊 for a low count) to add a touch of personality.
